## 1. Purpose and protected question

This standard defines the controlled evaluation, selection, pinning, configuration, isolation, qualification, operation, degradation, and removal of concrete TypeScript and analysis providers without allowing any provider portfolio to reshape the provider-independent semantic model. It turns README section 10.11 into a controlled qualification contract. It does not perform the qualification.

> For each exact capability and derived-fact type, which concrete provider is eligible for which bounded role, under which reproducible execution and trust boundary, with what retained provenance and loss, after what independently governed evidence, and under what health and removal conditions?

The cataloged control and qualification requirements make the answer false-green resistant. Candidate capability descriptions are unverified provider-independent HYPOTHESIS role proposals and qualification questions; excluded network lookup observations provide them no evidentiary support. A selected role exists only after the exact hard-five baseline, exact qualification evidence, applicable license and confidentiality decisions, and the required distinct authority all support that bounded use.

## 5. Provider-role model

| Role | Permitted contribution | Mandatory boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary extractor | Direct provider-specific observations for a declared capability and fact type | Declared perimeter, support basis, unsupported regions, raw trace, no independent authority |
| Primary normalizer | Map exact raw evidence into the controlled adapter contract | Preserve raw identity, loss, uncertainty, disagreements, and provider-specific extensions |
| Corroborator | Independently attributed supporting or conflicting observation | Shared-engine correlation declared; no silent overwrite or majority truth |
| Fallback | Bounded alternative after an exact trigger | Explicit degraded state, coverage delta, semantic delta, and blocked claims |
| Runner or collector | Produce exact test, coverage, or trace execution evidence | Remain distinct from oracle, semantic owner, and correctness conclusion |
| Store or query engine | Persist and retrieve exact normalized and raw evidence | No extraction, normalization, semantic, or authority role by storage convenience |

The role requirements assign the eventual provider matrix per capability and derived-fact type rather than per brand. A tool occupies several roles only through separately qualified rows. A provider with no row has no role. A capability with no qualified row is unsupported.

## 8. Decision 1 — role designation

A qualification plan identifies capability and derived-fact type first, then proposes one or more bounded role rows. Primary extraction and primary normalization are separate when the proposed portfolio assigns them separately. Corroboration strength is reduced when candidates share an engine, parser, rules, source transformation, fixture, or dependency. Fallback does not mean silent substitution: activation and evidence strength remain visible.

---

## 9. Decisions 2 and 3 — overlap, precedence, disagreement, and deduplication

Conflict precedence is claim-specific. Governing semantic meaning and an exact eligible oracle judge observations; provider popularity and configuration order do not. Exact compiler confirmation constrains claims labeled compiler-confirmed, but wrapper, heuristic, and corroborating outputs remain attributed. A subject or configuration mismatch is diagnosed before semantic disagreement is claimed.

Deduplication creates a logical group, never destructive replacement. Each member retains raw identity, provider, version, method, configuration, subject, locations, message, severity, confidence, limitations, health, time, and raw evidence. A group result cannot be greener than its unresolved material members permit.

---

## 10. Provenance, loss, and normalization contract

The normalization boundary is adversarial. It validates subject and adapter compatibility, preserves raw evidence or an auditable content address, emits explicit loss classifications, retains provider extensions, and refuses mixed-subject or provenance-deficient input. Generated and virtual code are never reported as authored source. Static and execution evidence remain separate identities even when correlated.

---

## 11. Decision 4 — version pinning and reproducibility

A reproducible run binds exact binaries or immutable distributions, transitive engines and plugins, compilers and runtimes, rule and query packs, configurations and defaults, environment, subject, generated context, caches, execution profile, adapter, and output schema. A lock entry is one input and does not prove installation. Floating services, tags, remote rules, or auto-configuration cannot support a frozen qualification.

---

## 12. Decision 5 — local, CI, isolated, and external execution profiles

| Profile | Default purpose | Binding limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local read-only | Early development feedback and bounded reproduction | No implicit install, write, network, secret, or binding-gate claim |
| CI controlled | Reproducible integration evidence | Immutable inputs, recognized authority context, controlled secrets and caches, exact artifacts |
| Isolated hostile-repository | Qualification and higher-risk analysis | Filesystem, process, network, secret, resource, and output sandbox; cleanup mandatory |
| External service or SaaS | Separately approved remote capability | Full data-flow, contract, retention, tenancy, region, availability, procurement, and exit review |

---

## 13. Decision 6 — licensing and redistribution

License assessment is component- and use-specific. Query source does not license a CLI; an engine does not license rule content; a server license does not necessarily govern every analyzer; community and commercial editions are not interchangeable. Closed-source analysis, automated CI, coding-agent integration, internal redistribution, external processing, and derivative rule distribution are separate questions. Ambiguity is a blocker pending accountable legal, procurement, and security review; this Draft supplies no legal advice or disposition.

---

## 14. Decisions 7 and 8 — sandbox, network, and source confidentiality

Network access defaults to denied. Repository code and executable configuration are hostile input. Provider execution receives only its declared filesystem, process, resource, secret, and network capabilities. Source, symbols, paths, source maps, coverage, caches, reports, screenshots, DOM snapshots, console output, request metadata, headers, bodies, and trace artifacts inherit applicable confidentiality. Telemetry, crash reporting, update checks, remote configuration, rules download, and uploads are separate data flows and require explicit evidence and authority.

---

## 15. Decision 9 — installation and update

Installation follows technical and nontechnical qualification planning; it is not an exploratory default. Exact source, integrity, dependency closure, scripts, native modules, binaries, runtimes, browser bundles, license evidence, sandbox, rollback, and cleanup are known before mutation. Automatic update is disabled for qualified providers. Every conclusion-affecting update creates a candidate successor and affected qualification; urgent disablement creates an explicit unsupported capability state rather than an unqualified silent replacement.

---

## 16. Decision 10 — performance budgets

This Draft decides the budget schema, measurement method, and admission policy. Budgets cover wall and CPU time, memory, disk, files, processes, output, and network at declared subject scales for clean full, warm full, incremental, changed closure, and adversarial cases. Measurements retain distributions and outliers. Incremental speed requires clean-full equivalence. Timeout or resource refusal is non-green. Performance never justifies provenance loss, disabled checks, weakened oracles, or an unsupported substitution. Exact numerical ceilings for each provider, role, and subject scale remain OPEN and are mandatory qualification inputs before selection.

---

## 17. Decision 11 — provider health and degraded behavior

Health is orthogonal to finding outcome. A usable result requires exact executable identity, startup and version handshake, configuration and subject discovery, output parsing, positive completeness markers, resource compatibility, and controlled termination. Degraded, partial, stale, unsupported, failed, cancelled, timed-out, malformed, and unavailable are distinct. Fallback activation retains the primary failure and its coverage and semantic delta. No zero-result or zero-exit shortcut creates green.

---

## 18. Decision 12 — qualification criteria and record

Every qualification binds an exact role and uses only exact eligible fixture judgments and executable methods. The record includes identity, capabilities, fact types, subject class, environment, profile, security and confidentiality evidence, licenses, performance, health, raw and normalized output, loss, false results, disagreement, incremental equivalence, gaps, decisions, approvals, expiry, and removal triggers. A PASS is narrow and version-bound; this Draft contains no such record.

| Qualification predicate | Mandatory evidence | Fail-closed condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic fit | Exact capability and fact-type cases against eligible 006 judgments and 008 methods | Unsupported, lossy, provenance-deficient, vacuous, or materially incorrect |
| Differential behavior | Attributed provider outputs and shared-dependency account | Hidden disagreement or desired-result precedence |
| Incremental behavior | Clean full and incremental equivalence over exact change scenarios | Stale, mixed, missed, or spurious results beyond criterion |
| Security and confidentiality | Hostile repository, sandbox, secret canary, network denial, telemetry, and artifact handling | Escape, disclosure, undeclared execution, or uncontrolled data flow |
| Licensing and operations | Exact terms, intended use, installation, update, health, performance, recovery, and removal evidence | Unresolved rights or operational predicate |
| Nonvacuity | Positive population, negative controls, mutations, and no-empty-analyzer defenses | Empty output accepted without coverage proof |

---

## 19. Removal, replacement, and retained evidence

Removal quarantines new use, identifies dependent facts and gates, preserves historical attributed evidence subject to controlled retention, invalidates current claims, and triggers reanalysis. A replacement receives its own qualification. Rollback is permitted only when the earlier exact qualification remains applicable. Emergency disablement is immediate when separately authorized, and the capability becomes explicitly unavailable or degraded.
