## 1. Two corrections to the design, from the mechanics pass

⛔ **DESIGN §6 ITEM 4 IS MIS-SHAPED, AND THE SHAPE MATTERS FOR WHAT GETS RATIFIED.** It asks *"whether workspace
content is PER-8 participating material."* **PER-8 classifies per OBJECT by a PAST-TENSE ACT**, not per content
kind. So the ratifiable question is not *"are workspace files participating material"* but *"which object, having
done what, carries this content."* Reshaped in §10.

⛔ **`Workspace.purge` CANNOT MIRROR `ArtifactStore.purge` EXACTLY, AND I ASSERTED IT COULD.** The store's
purgeability class **is part of the storage key** —
`` `${input.tenantPrefix}/${input.purgeability}/${contentHash.slice(7)}` `` — which is how one flat key space
keeps retention legible. **A workspace is path-named, not content-addressed, so it cannot carry the class in its
address.** The mirror is therefore partial by construction: same `purge(address) → PurgeOutcome` signature, same
tombstone rule, and a *declared* rather than *addressed* purgeability. That difference is designed in §3, not
assumed away.

⭑ What DOES carry over verbatim, and must: `purge` takes one argument and no actor, clock or reason
(`ports/artifact-store.ts:107`); the semantics are **"Remove the BYTES and keep the ENTRY"**, because
*"an `ARTIFACT` record referencing a purged key must stay EXPLICABLE"*; and `Purgeability` has exactly two
members and is **"DELIBERATELY NOT A RETENTION CLASS"** (`REG-Q-056` is OPEN). ⛔ **Do not coin a third member
and do not add a retention class here.**

⭐ **THE CONTAINMENT DEFECT IS CLOSED AT THE SIGNATURE, NOT AT THE CALL SITE.** `OpenProducerSession` now takes a

the inverse orphan `ArtifactStore.durability` exists to prevent (*"a permanent record names a `storageKey`
whose store answer, after one restart, is byte-indistinguishable from a key that was never stored"*).
`getArtifactStore()` already derives the content plane from the record plane's key; the workspace root derives
the same way (`${DB_PATH}.workspaces/<undertakingId>`). One key configures all three planes or none.

are written, files are read — so each needed an assertion of its own. ⚠ **`P2-k` SURVIVED THE FIRST RUN**: the
purgeability declaration was untested, and the only thing that exposed it was asking the workspace to DESTROY
the product and requiring it to refuse. That is the instrument doing its job, recorded rather than quietly
fixed.

on schedule" as distinct from "never written" and from "lost". The entry therefore lives in a record beside the
files, and that record is a RESERVED path: a step that could write it could rewrite the retention history of
everything else in the workspace. ⭑ Its refusal is DISTINCT from the containment refusal, because a caller
naming the record has not tried to escape and telling it that it did would be false.

## 5. Phase 3 · Capability → tools, by absence

**DO:** thread the step's GRANTED capabilities into the session and mint only the authorised tools.
`stepBindingFor` stops hardcoding `[{capability:'network'}]` and requests **per step type**, splitting
capabilities a step **REQUIRES** (ungranted ⇒ refuse the step) from ones it **USES** (ungranted ⇒ do not mint
the tool).
