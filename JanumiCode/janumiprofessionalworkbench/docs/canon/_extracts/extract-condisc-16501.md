## CONSTITUTIONAL CANDIDATES

- [ASSISTANT] "The Janumi Execution Model, or JEM, defines the invariant behavioral semantics by which Janumi professional cognition becomes executable." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16526) — JEM's charter sentence: execution is the bridge from cognition to authority, and its semantics are invariant.
- [ASSISTANT] "The runtime profile may vary. The execution semantics SHALL not. ... A runtime is Janumi-conformant only if it preserves the semantics defined in this specification." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16556-16576) — Conformance axiom: meaning-preservation, not technology, defines what counts as a Janumi runtime.
- [ASSISTANT] "Nothing is authoritative merely because: an agent generated output; a workflow step completed; a UI submitted a form; an external tool returned successfully; an event was placed on a message bus; ... a human expressed informal approval." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16594-16603) — The Central Execution Principle's negative form: authority comes only from the governed commit path, never from activity or informal assent.
- [ASSISTANT] "JEM SHALL distinguish: computational activity; professional progress." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17728-17731) — First-principle split: tokens/tool-calls/files are not progress; only uncertainty reduction, evidence, decision readiness, and synthesis are.
- [ASSISTANT] "AI output SHALL be treated as proposed professional state unless the AI possesses explicit governed authority for the relevant Command." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17575-17577) — The proposal boundary: AI participation is propose-by-default; authority is an explicit governed grant, never implicit.

## DOCTRINE-CONOP

- [ASSISTANT] "A Janumi state change becomes authoritative only when: a semantic Command has been received; the caller has been authenticated; authority has been evaluated; ... immutable semantic Events have been persisted; the authoritative state has been committed atomically within the declared consistency boundary." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16582-16592) — The nine-condition positive path to authority; everything in the runtime CONOP hangs off this sequence.
- [ASSISTANT] "Authority SHALL be evaluated at Command execution time. It SHALL not be trusted solely from: UI visibility; stale access tokens; prior role assignment; agent prompt instructions; cached projection metadata." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16800-16810) — Authority is live, not cached; prompts and UI affordances are explicitly non-authoritative sources.
- [ASSISTANT] "An AI request SHALL not inherit unrestricted authority from the service account executing it." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16796) — Agent identity is first-class; infrastructure identity never launders into professional authority.
- [ASSISTANT] "Where multiple approvals or quorum are required, one approval SHALL produce an intermediate authoritative state rather than final approval." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16841-16843) — Multi-party governance is modeled as real intermediate states, not accumulated flags.
- [ASSISTANT] "An inconclusive Validator result SHALL not be silently treated as pass." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17035-17037) — Fail-closed epistemics: the absence of a verdict is itself a governed condition, with declared disposition.
- [ASSISTANT] "A Command requiring asynchronous validation SHALL not falsely complete. It SHALL instead produce an appropriate intermediate state ... and a durable Process Instance." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17086-17096) — Honest state doctrine: pending work is represented as pending, never as done.
- [ASSISTANT] "Waiting for: human review; external Evidence; time; ... SHALL be represented durably, not by keeping an in-memory thread alive." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17373-17385) — Professional work waits for weeks; durability of waiting is a CONOP requirement, not an optimization.
- [ASSISTANT] "The runtime SHALL not require storage of private model chain-of-thought. It SHALL preserve professional rationale, Evidence, assumptions, methods, and limitations sufficient for review." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17594-17598) — Reviewability doctrine: professional rationale is the accountable record; private CoT is out of scope.
- [ASSISTANT] "Retries SHALL distinguish transient execution failure from professional reasoning failure. ... This SHALL not be automatic without policy because repetition may provide no professional value." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17634-17652) — Technical retry vs professional retry: re-running reasoning is a professional act, not plumbing.
- [ASSISTANT] "A tactic change is not a retry. It changes the professional approach." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17675-17677) — Core CPCO-loop move: when a method stalls, change method/agent/framing under coordination authority, with preserved tactic history.
- [ASSISTANT] "No-progress detection SHOULD trigger tactic evaluation, not immediate blind retry." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17767) — Operational corollary of the activity/progress split: stall handling is deliberative.
- [ASSISTANT] "Resumption SHALL re-evaluate: authority; current semantic state; relevant Constraints; stale assumptions; ... A suspended Process SHALL not assume that the world remained unchanged." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17808-17820) — Resumption is re-grounding, not continuation; time invalidates context.
- [ASSISTANT] "A compensation is a new professional or technical Action. It is not a rollback of history." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17897-17901) — History is immutable; undo is forward-moving professional work with its own authority and residual effect.
- [ASSISTANT] "External operation success SHALL not automatically imply: Decision correctness; Action validation; outcome achievement; PWU completion." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17882-17889) — Integration doctrine: external system results are evidence, never professional conclusions.
- [ASSISTANT] "Reconciliation SHALL be a first-class Process. ... Applying reconciliation SHALL issue normal semantic Commands. The reconciliation Process SHALL not bypass Aggregate invariants." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17924-17977) — Incoherence resolution goes through the same governed front door as all other change.

## SEMANTIC-INVARIANTS

- [ASSISTANT] "Material professional state SHALL not use silent last-write-wins behavior." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16901-16903) — Concurrency invariant: conflicting professional edits surface for revalidation or reconciliation, never silent overwrite.
- [ASSISTANT] "It SHALL not automatically resubmit a stale material Command without revalidation." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16915) — Companion invariant: after a version conflict, intent must be re-formed against current state.
- [ASSISTANT] "Normalization SHALL not change professional intent." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16960) — Input cleanup is semantically inert; intent is inviolable across the boundary.
- [ASSISTANT] "All external inputs are untrusted. The runtime SHALL: parse normalize validate canonicalize authorize before authoritative mutation." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16964-16974) — The boundary contract: a fixed trust pipeline stands between the world and authoritative state.
- [ASSISTANT] "A Validator SHALL not directly mutate authoritative domain state. ... Authoritative changes require Commands." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17047-17059) — Evaluation and mutation are separated powers; validators observe and propose only.
- [ASSISTANT] "An Event is authoritative because it was committed, not because it was delivered." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17236) — Truth lives at the commit point; messaging is transport, not authority.
- [ASSISTANT] "A snapshot is an optimization, not an independent truth." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16874) — Derived state never competes with the event/aggregate record for authority.
- [ASSISTANT] "`occurredAt` represents semantic occurrence. `recordedAt` represents runtime persistence. These SHALL remain distinct." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17207-17213) — Professional time vs machine time is a preserved semantic distinction.
- [ASSISTANT] "Predictive Projection: Derived from models and explicitly non-authoritative. Every projection SHALL disclose its consistency mode." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17311-17315) — Projections are honest about what kind of truth they show, including model-generated speculation.
- [ASSISTANT] "A projection SHALL be rebuildable from authoritative state and Event history. A projection that cannot be rebuilt SHALL be treated as an authoritative subsystem and governed separately." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17281-17285) — Derived-ness is testable: if you can't rebuild it, it holds truth and must be governed as such.
- [ASSISTANT] "An RPH may be: observing while its coordinated PWUs occupy many different lifecycle states." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17412-17418) — Coordination state and work state are orthogonal axes; RPH state is never a rollup of PWU states.
- [ASSISTANT] "The plan does not directly mutate PWUs. It produces or proposes semantic Commands." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17474-17479) — Planning is upstream of authority; even the coordinator's own plan goes through Commands.
- [ASSISTANT] "An Agent Execution may complete successfully while the PWU remains incomplete." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17617-17619) — Execution success ≠ professional completion (the exec≠assurance separation at runtime).
- [ASSISTANT] "JEM does not require distributed ACID transactions across all professional state. Cross-Aggregate coherence SHALL be maintained through: Commands; Events; Process Instances; Validators" (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L17990-17999) — Coherence is a governed professional process, not a database guarantee; global lockstep is explicitly rejected.

## PROTOCOL-PRACTICE

- [ASSISTANT] "validators SHALL execute in this sequence: structural; type and schema; authority-independent semantic; governance; security and safety; aggregate invariants; cross-aggregate coherence; external; AI-assisted; human validation. This order minimizes expensive or external work on obviously invalid Commands." (Janumi Constitution Discussion.md L16994-17009) — Cheap-to-expensive validator ordering as default practice; human attention is the most protected resource, spent last.
