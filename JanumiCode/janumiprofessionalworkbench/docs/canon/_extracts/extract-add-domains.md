## CONSTITUTIONAL CANDIDATES

- [ASSISTANT] "The Professional Work Unit may be a common primitive, but professional domains compose those units into different work topologies. The RPH must preserve the topology of the profession rather than forcing every profession into the same agent workflow." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L249) — The domain-general thesis stated at constitutional strength: one primitive, many topologies; the harness must not flatten professions into one workflow.
- [ASSISTANT] "SOFTWARE → recursive decomposition LEGAL → interdependent reinterpretation HEALTHCARE → evidence-driven hypothesis revision CONSTRUCTION → distributed coordination and state reconciliation" (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L242-245) — The four-domain topology catalog: each domain teaches a distinct structural property of the same RPH.
- [ASSISTANT] "How does a complex, distributed professional undertaking preserve intent, state, evidence, dependencies, accountability, and verification across thousands of interdependent units of work performed by different organizations over months or years?" (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L13) — Canonical domain-independent problem statement RPH exists to answer; enumerates the axes every PWU must preserve.
- [ASSISTANT] "Much of professional administration is the machinery by which complex work maintains coherence. AI should not merely automate the paperwork. It should participate in the control system the paperwork imperfectly implements." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L182) — Constitutional reframe: administration is the control system of professional work; RPH's role is control-system participation, not clerical automation.
- [ASSISTANT] "the PWU does not only produce evidence for verification; it also produces state transitions that may authorize or block dependent work. That is a significant addition to the canonical PWU model." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L251) — Proposed amendment to the canonical PWU model: verification outcomes are authorizing/blocking state transitions, not just evidence.
- [ASSISTANT] "both systems address the same abstract problem: Large, messy, underspecified evidence space → semantic reconstruction → competing hypotheses → recursive evidence acquisition → adversarial validation → professionally usable artifact" (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L304-321) — The shared abstract pipeline underlying JanumiCode and JanumiHealth; direct structural evidence of domain independence.
- [ASSISTANT] "Healthcare is organized around encounters, tests and specialties. Many difficult diagnostic failures exist in the gaps between them. JanumiHealth could be designed to own the unresolved case as a persistent work object." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L343) — Persistent-work-object thesis: professional failure lives in the gaps between institutional units; the harness owns the long-lived case.
- [ASSISTANT] "This development strongly validates your premise that the reusable enterprise unit should shift from the complete application to a professional capability." (Google Atomic Agents Discussion.md L208) — External corroboration (Google's Atomic Agents) of the capability-as-unit-of-reuse premise underlying the PWU model.
- [ASSISTANT] "Atomic Agents would fit inside the Janumi model. They would be reusable executors or capability providers employed by Professional Work Architecture." (Google Atomic Agents Discussion.md L236) — Layering ruling: third-party agent capabilities are executors under PWA governance, never a replacement for the harness.
- [ASSISTANT] "Janumi's differentiated territory remains the control system above those capabilities—the system that continuously maintains professional coherence across outcomes, artifacts, organizational functions, and changing enterprise reality." (Google Atomic Agents Discussion.md L238) — Identity boundary: Janumi = the coherence-maintaining control plane above capabilities; capabilities themselves are commoditizing.

## DOCTRINE-CONOP

- [ASSISTANT] "The paperwork is not the work. But the paperwork preserves the state of the work. The AI opportunity is not just filling out paperwork faster. It is continuously reconciling professional state across fragmented repositories." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L562-566) — Core CONOP: professional documents are state carriers; the product is state reconciliation, not document automation.
- [ASSISTANT] "The RPH would not own all artifacts. It would integrate with systems where professional evidence already lives." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L277) — Integration doctrine: RPH is a reconciliation layer over external systems of record, not a universal repository.
- [ASSISTANT] "The RPH continuously asks: What was intended? What was approved? What is currently true? What has changed? What evidence exists? What depends on this? What can proceed? What must be reopened?" (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L370-379) — The canonical continuous-reconciliation question set; the operational heart of RPH across all domains.
- [ASSISTANT] "Traditional task tracking often treats conflict as a note, comment, or blocker. RPH treats it as a reason to reopen the relevant professional work unit." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L456-458) — Reopening doctrine: contradicting evidence reopens work as a governed state change, with impact propagated to dependent PWUs.
- [ASSISTANT] "That is not merely automation. It is professional coherence management." (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L547) — Names the product category RPH occupies; distinguishes it from dashboards and task automation.
- [ASSISTANT] "The business concept I see is not an AI doctor and not primarily a diagnostic chatbot. It is a longitudinal diagnostic investigation system." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L14-17) — Positioning doctrine: the harness produces governed investigations, not oracle answers — the same stance across every domain.
- [ASSISTANT] "What clinically plausible explanations remain insufficiently investigated when all of this person's evidence is considered together over time? … The second invites an LLM to guess. The first defines a rigorous professional work unit." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L22-28) — Question-framing doctrine: a well-posed professional question defines a PWU; an ill-posed one invites model guessing.
- [ASSISTANT] "A single LLM prompt containing 300 pages of medical records would be the wrong architecture. It would recreate the exact weaknesses you have identified in coding agents: shortcut-taking, premature convergence, identity loss, shallow verification and long-horizon degradation." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L195) — Anti-monolith doctrine: the failure modes motivating decomposition are model failure modes, hence domain-independent.
- [ASSISTANT] "The important part is that these should not merely vote. They should operate against a shared case ontology and produce explicit evidence-bearing artifacts." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L214-216) — Multi-unit coordination doctrine: shared ontology plus evidence-bearing artifacts, not ensemble voting.
- [ASSISTANT] "The reusable core must be separated from domain-specific policy, ontology, and evidence requirements." (Google Atomic Agents Discussion.md L190) — Direct architectural statement of the domain-general thesis: generic capability core, domain content injected as policy/ontology/evidence.

## SEMANTIC-INVARIANTS

- [ASSISTANT] "A collection of individually non-alarming findings does not constitute an explanation for a persistent phenotype." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L80) — Invariant: item-level acceptability never implies case-level explanation; verification must be holistic over the evidence set.
- [ASSISTANT] "So I would make unexplainedness itself a first-class state variable in JanumiHealth." (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L84) — Unexplained residual is explicit governed state, not the absence of a finding; generalizes to any domain's open questions.
- [ASSISTANT] "the case should not reach a state of `RESOLVED`. It should remain: PARTIALLY_EXPLAINED" (Janumi and Healthcare Discussion.md L183-187) — Lifecycle invariant: partial coverage of intent by accepted conclusions blocks the resolved state; the semantic rule, not the enum spelling, is load-bearing.
- [ASSISTANT] "INSPECTION … evaluates executed work … accepts or rejects state … gates dependent work" (Janumi in the Construction Field Discussion.md L163-167) — Verification acts gate dependent work; assurance outcomes are authorization events in the dependency graph, not annotations.
- [ASSISTANT] "A reusable agent needs stronger contracts than a reusable software function because semantically plausible but incompatible output can still satisfy a JSON schema. Structural validation must be complemented by semantic invariants, evidence requirements, uncertainty thresholds, and downstream acceptance tests." (Google Atomic Agents Discussion.md L198) — Schema conformance is insufficient; capability contracts must carry semantic invariants and evidence requirements — the rationale for JPWB's assurance layer.
