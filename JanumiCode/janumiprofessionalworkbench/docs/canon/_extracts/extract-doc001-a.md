## CONSTITUTIONAL CANDIDATES

- "Janumi Professional Workbench will not merely execute an AI Execution Workflow. It will preserve and assure the user’s intent as professional work is recursively transformed into a verified software outcome." (RPH-DOC-001.md L149-L151) — The central product promise; the single sentence everything else in the migration serves.
- "All work must remain traceable to the originating user intent or to a later authorized intent revision. The decomposition exists to serve the intent. The intent does not exist to stabilize the decomposition." (RPH-DOC-001.md L206-L212) — §5.1 Intent is authoritative; establishes intent as the root of all traceability.
- "An Execution Workflow is a temporal execution projection. It is not the authoritative definition of professional work. The same PWU may be executed using different plans, agents, models, tools, or sequencing strategies without changing the identity of the work." (RPH-DOC-001.md L216-L220) — §5.2; workflow demoted to projection; work identity invariant under strategy substitution.
- "A model or tool may successfully produce an output while the professional obligation remains unsatisfied. Execution and assurance must use separate states." (RPH-DOC-001.md L222-L226) — §5.3; exec≠assurance, the invariant later ratified as INV-5.
- "When a parent PWU is decomposed into children, the system is making a claim that the children collectively cover the parent obligation. That claim must be explicit and, for material work, validated." (RPH-DOC-001.md L230-L232) — §5.4 Decomposition is a claim, not a bookkeeping act.
- "Outputs are not automatically evidence." (RPH-DOC-001.md L236) — §5.5; blocks the vacuous move of treating any artifact as proof.
- "Assurance is not confined to the legacy final `VALIDATE` phase." (RPH-DOC-001.md L249) — §5.6; assurance is continuous and may attach at seven listed points from pre-decomposition to pre-promotion (L251-L259).
- "Replanning and reshaping are control operations, not terminal legacy workflow phases." (RPH-DOC-001.md L263) — §5.7; reshaping may occur anywhere, triggered by falsified assumptions, constraint changes, infeasibility (L265-L273).
- "A PWU or template may request a capability. Only the runtime policy system may grant" tool/file/network/secret/command/model/sandbox access. (RPH-DOC-001.md L277-L287) — §5.8; runtime authority separated from work definition — the request-vs-grant security boundary.
- "The amount of structure and assurance applied to a PWU should reflect: consequence; uncertainty; irreversibility; regulatory exposure; security sensitivity; organizational policy." (RPH-DOC-001.md L289-L298) — §5.9 proportionality; forbids uniform heavyweight ceremony.

## DOCTRINE-CONOP

- "professional work definition, execution sequencing, agent assignment, validation, governance, and state progression are encoded together in one linear legacy phase machine." (RPH-DOC-001.md L29) — The single-sentence diagnosis of why the legacy Product Lens must be replaced.
- "The RPH does not treat an Execution Workflow as the authoritative representation of an Undertaking. Instead, it represents the Undertaking as a Professional Work Graph of persistent Professional Work Objects, including executable Professional Work Units" (RPH-DOC-001.md L33) — What RPH is: graph of persistent objects, not a phase machine.
- Six separated concerns: "Prompt Engineering formulates model interactions... Context Engineering assembles information... Harness Engineering provides tools, models, sandboxes... Loop Engineering determines how execution progresses... Shape Engineering defines the professional work... Assurance Engineering establishes justified confidence" (RPH-DOC-001.md L46-L53) — The canonical six-discipline separation the entire architecture is organized around.
- "The RPH joins these stacks. It contains the persistent Professional Work Objects while the execution stack acts on them and the assurance system measures whether the work remains within acceptable semantic and operational bounds." (RPH-DOC-001.md L302-L417) — Two complementary stacks (Execution: Prompt/Context/Harness/Loop; Professional Work: Shape/Assurance) joined by RPH as custodian of persistent objects.
- Five limitations of the legacy coupling: "Rigidity... Semantic ambiguity... Weak recursive semantics... Completion conflation... Limited adaptability" (RPH-DOC-001.md L81-L118) — The problem taxonomy; each maps to a principle in §5.
- "Janumi Professional Workbench will become a Shape Engineering workbench and execution runtime for governed professional software development." (RPH-DOC-001.md L123) — Product vision sentence; names Shape Engineering as the product's identity.
- Runtime loop: "1. shapes the work; 2. generates or selects an execution strategy; 3. binds agents, models, tools, permissions, and context; 4. executes... 5. captures evidence; 6. performs assurance; 7. decides whether to accept, retry, reshape, replan, escalate, or abandon; 8. recomposes... 9. promotes accepted results into an authoritative baseline." (RPH-DOC-001.md L137-L147) — Canonical nine-step operating rhythm of the system.
- "The initial migration will preserve the effective behavior of the legacy Product Lens... prove semantic and behavioral parity with the legacy orchestrator; create the foundation for later dynamic decomposition" (RPH-DOC-001.md L55-L61) — Migration CONOP: parity-first, dynamism deferred.
- Deferred: "unrestricted natural-language generation of arbitrary RPH structures... fully editable execution graphs; autonomous changes to runtime privileges" (L178-L189); non-goals: not "a DAG runner with AI nodes; a project management system; an unrestricted autonomous coding system." (RPH-DOC-001.md L178-L200) — Scope boundary and identity-by-negation; guards against the likeliest misreadings.
- "Legacy phases will not be mechanically converted into generic Execution Workflow nodes. Each legacy phase will be reclassified as one or more of: PWU; execution strategy; assurance policy; governance decision; baseline transition; control operation." (RPH-DOC-001.md L839-L848) — §9.1 migration principle: semantic reclassification, not node translation.
- "`DISCUSSING` is not a professional completion state. It is an interaction mode." — likewise SYNTHESIZING=execution state, AWAITING_APPROVAL=governance state, CLARIFYING="a control operation that can occur whenever required information is missing." (RPH-DOC-001.md L874-L882) — INTAKE substates disentangled into four distinct semantic categories.
- "Proposal is a work-producing activity. It must not automatically make its assumptions authoritative." (RPH-DOC-001.md L916-L918) — PROPOSE reclassification; proposer output stays non-authoritative until governed.
- ASSUMPTION_SURFACING "is no longer a mandatory global legacy phase in the canonical model. It is a cross-cutting assurance policy attached to material PWUs and execution outputs." (RPH-DOC-001.md L932) — The exemplar phase-to-policy reclassification.
- "Review is not represented as an ordinary legacy execution phase in the canonical model. It is a governance event triggered by policy." (RPH-DOC-001.md L983) — Human review moves from sequence position to policy trigger.
- "The MAKER system becomes one execution strategy available to appropriate PWUs." (RPH-DOC-001.md L1000) — Demotes the legacy agent system from the pipeline to a pluggable strategy; similarly VALIDATE dissolves into a continuous policy-driven assurance catalog (L1008-L1022).
- "A repository commit is a technical configuration-management operation. Baseline promotion is a separate governance operation and must not be inferred from commit success." (RPH-DOC-001.md L1036) — COMMIT split; promotion is a decision, never an inference.
- "REPLAN is removed as a terminal phase from the canonical model and retained only as a legacy compatibility label." (RPH-DOC-001.md L1051) — Replanning becomes "Control action available from multiple states" (L1044) with descendant invalidation and decision record.

## VOCABULARY

- "A Professional Work Object, or PWO, is any persistent professional entity with identity, provenance, lifecycle, and relationships." (RPH-DOC-001.md L425) — Root noun of the domain model; examples span intent through execution plan (L427-L442).
- "A PWU is an executable Professional Work Object." (RPH-DOC-001.md L467) — The defining relation between PWU and PWO in five words.
- Each PWU carries: "why the work exists; what it must accomplish; what constraints it inherits; what assumptions govern it; what evidence it must produce; how its success will be verified; how its result contributes to its parent objective." (RPH-DOC-001.md L127-L135) — Meaning-level PWU content definition, independent of any schema.
- Architecture "should be modeled as a decomposable PWU Type hierarchy that becomes a concrete PWU Instance hierarchy in an Undertaking." (RPH-DOC-001.md L900) — Establishes the Type-vs-Instance distinction with the Undertaking as instantiation scope.

## SEMANTIC-INVARIANTS

- "Evidence must identify: the claim it supports; its provenance; its scope; its limitations; its validity; its disposition." (RPH-DOC-001.md L238-L245) — Admissibility conditions for evidence; the semantic rule behind the Evidence object.
- Decomposition invariants: "every mandatory parent constraint must be propagated, retained, or explicitly waived; every parent obligation must be delegated, retained, or explicitly removed through an authorized decision... decomposition approval must be distinct from execution approval for material work." (RPH-DOC-001.md L798-L805) — Conservation laws for obligations and constraints across decomposition.
- "Recomposition is not merely concatenation. It must determine whether assured child results collectively satisfy the parent intent." (RPH-DOC-001.md L831) — Recomposition is a judged claim against parent intent, not aggregation.
