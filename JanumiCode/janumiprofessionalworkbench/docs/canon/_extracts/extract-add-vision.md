## CONSTITUTIONAL CANDIDATES

- "Professional work is not fundamentally a sequence of tasks or a collection of artifacts. It is the exercise of professional capability within a changing, uncertain, consequential reality." (Vision-Map.md L28) — The founding observation; every other concept in the corpus derives from this reframing.
- "The enduring asset is not only the output. It is the capability to produce, sustain, adapt, validate, and reproduce valuable outcomes under changing conditions." (Vision-Map.md L32) — Capability, not artifact, is the asset Janumi exists to steward.
- "To provide infrastructure through which humans and agents can deliberately shape, exercise, assure, preserve, and evolve professional capability across complex scenarios, organizational boundaries, technological change, and generations of time." (Vision-Map.md L36) — The single-sentence statement of Janumi's long-term purpose.
- "Janumi's ultimate concern is not simply whether an artifact was produced. It is whether humanity remains capable of producing, understanding, governing, maintaining, improving, and, when necessary, reconstituting the outcomes on which society depends." (Vision-Map.md L54) — Civilizational framing; reconstitution capability is the ultimate test (echoed for EUV/CERN/JWST at L773).
- "This is the practical meaning of the **irreversibility of reductionism**: understanding or completing every part does not guarantee recovery of the whole." (Vision-Map.md L79) — Names the failure mode the architecture (decomposition Claims, recomposition, Intent preservation, Assurance) answers (L81).
- "Time is therefore not metadata attached to professional state. It is one of the dimensions through which professional meaning, Evidence, authority, continuity, and capability must be understood." (Vision-Map.md L108) — Time as constitutive; grounds multi-time semantics (event/observation/knowledge/record/effective/validity/supersession, L104, L194).
- "This is why capability must be actively preserved. The continued existence of artifacts does not establish the continued existence of the ability that produced them." (Vision-Map.md L388) — Capability degrades independently of artifacts (degradation causes enumerated L377-386); preservation is an active obligation.
- "Janumi may help externalize and connect capability, but it must never assume that all professional knowledge can or should be captured." (Vision-Map.md L718) — Epistemic humility clause: tacit, embodied, relational knowledge stays partly outside the system (L704-716).
- "Janumi should not aspire to control civilization. It should aspire to make professional capability more explicit, inspectable, evidence-backed, composable, resilient, transferable, and capable of governed learning." (Vision-Map.md L777) — Stewardship-not-control; canonical formula "governed stewardship of professional capability across ... generations of time" (L781).
- "no scenario, PWA, capability declaration, agent, Validator, or registry grants itself authority." (Vision-Map.md L457) — No self-authorization, extended to every envisioned higher-order object; governance retains approve/waive/qualify authority (L456).
- "Janumi must not become: 1. An omniscient model of reality. ... 7. A self-authorizing professional actor. ... 9. A silent cross-client or cross-tenant learning system. ... 13. A replacement for professional judgment or democratic and institutional governance. ... 14. An assurance theater of scores and green badges." (Vision-Map.md L1162-1178) — Fifteen negative constitutional limits; the vision's own guardrails against its scale.
- "AI greatly expands the amount of professional activity that can be attempted. It does not automatically preserve Intent, truth, authority, wholeness, or capability. Without a stronger architecture, increased generation may produce increased fragmentation." (Vision-Map.md L1209) — The AI-era threat thesis Janumi answers.
- "The vision must be willing to learn that a proposed abstraction is unnecessary, incomplete, unsafe, or wrongly bounded. No theory becomes true because it is ambitious." (Vision-Map.md L1199) — Self-falsifiability: ten strategic hypotheses (L1184-1195) carry explicit evidence obligations (L1197).
- "Janumi seeks to help humanity move from producing isolated professional artifacts to deliberately stewarding professional capability—across complex scenarios, across institutions, across technological eras, and across time." (Vision-Map.md L1227) — The grand aspiration in final form; the arc artifact → capability → stewardship (recapped L1231).
- "This document describes an aspiration ... It is not an implementation constitution, wire contract, object registry, roadmap commitment, or authorization to add new canonical types or services." (Vision-Map.md L14) — Self-limiting authority clause: candidate terms influence implementation "only after deliberate semantic design, governance, contract evolution, migration planning, and conformance work" (L18).

## DOCTRINE-CONOP

- "The full vision is better understood as a cycle than as a top-down hierarchy" ... "Outcomes change the scenario, experience changes the Evidence for capability, and governed learning changes what future professional systems can do." (Vision-Map.md L114, L155) — The regenerative cycle: civilizational knowledge → systems → capabilities → scenarios → PWAs → Undertakings → PWUs → Outcomes → learning, looping (L116-137).
- "A Professional Scenario expresses situated professional demand. A Professional Capability expresses conditional professional ability. A Professional System realizes that ability. Shape Engineering creates or evolves PWAs that organize its application. An Undertaking commits selected capability to a specific scenario." (Vision-Map.md L404) — How vision layers relate to the existing canonical chain; they "do not replace or collapse that chain" (L420); relationships are many-to-many (L422-430).
- "A registry declaration may aid discovery, but policy-governed resolution determines eligibility. Execution success does not prove capability or authorize downstream effect." (Vision-Map.md L446) — Demand≠supply doctrine: declaration, resolution, binding, and assurance are distinct stages (L887-901); resolution must be inspectable, never "a magical self-optimizing mesh" (L948).
- "JanumiCode is the first demanding existence proof. If Shape Engineering can reliably outperform prompt engineering, ad hoc agent orchestration, and artifact-centered automation in software engineering, the method becomes credible for additional professional domains." (Vision-Map.md L810) — Commercialization doctrine: Shape Engineering (defined L789) is the entry layer; near-term business and long-term vision reinforce without "a speculative pivot" (L808).
- "The universal layer supplies a grammar for professional work; domain ontologies and PWAs supply professional meaning." (Vision-Map.md L816) — Universal-grammar doctrine; "Universal semantics provide a grammar, not every professional noun" (L1016); domain semantics extend, never replace (L1032); PWUs remain the common primitive across domain topologies (L864).
- "Core correctness and trustworthy assurance must not be reserved for enterprise customers." ... "Paid tiers monetize managed scale, curation, integration, federation, and organizational governance—not permission to be trustworthy." (Vision-Map.md L967, L1000) — Tiering doctrine: open-source keeps full de minimis assurance and durable Evidence (L965).
- "These horizons express direction, not promised dates or implementation phases." (Vision-Map.md L1114) — VISION: seven horizons — H1 prove Shape Engineering, H2 capability supply, H3 Professional Scenario, H4 domain families (Legal/Construction/Health), H5 capability portfolios and governed learning, H6 federation, H7 civilizational stewardship (L1116-1154); H7 is "a directional north star, not a product claim" whose purpose is that near-term architecture "does not foreclose long-term stewardship" (L1154). Federation favors selective disclosure and attestations "over a universal central database" (L739).

## VOCABULARY

- "A Professional Scenario is an identity-bearing, time-evolving, epistemically qualified, authority- and access-bounded representation of a consequential situation across its relevant past, present, and possible future states ... while remaining explicitly distinct from external reality." (Vision-Map.md L163) — Candidate definition; the situated-demand side; exists only when consequence/uncertainty/responsibility suffice to sponsor Undertakings (L221).
- "A Professional System is the socio-technical arrangement of people, organizations, knowledge, institutions, tools, infrastructure, relationships, authority, work architectures, and governance through which Professional Capabilities are realized." (Vision-Map.md L284) — Candidate definition; "The bearer of capability is not a document, model, PWA, or agent" (L286).
- "A Professional Capability is an evidence-backed, versioned, temporally qualified, and governable ability of a Professional System or federation of systems to transform a defined class of Professional Scenarios toward intended Outcomes under declared conditions ... with demonstrated repeatability, adaptability, and resilience." (Vision-Map.md L294) — Candidate definition; "No professional capability exists without conditions" (L300); twelve descriptive facets (L327-338).
- "A Civilizational Capability is a durable, distributed, and intergenerational ability of a societal ecosystem to produce, sustain, adapt, or reconstitute a consequential class of Outcomes across time, despite turnover or discontinuity in its people, organizations, institutions, knowledge custodians, infrastructure, and technologies." (Vision-Map.md L690) — Candidate definition; no single owner (L698); identity survives dormancy and permits reconstitution (L743-745).
- "Capability | Demonstrated ability ... Competence | Knowledge, skill, or qualification of a contributor. Capacity | Amount of work the system can currently support. ... Authority | Whether an actor may lawfully or professionally act or decide. Readiness | Whether capability can be mobilized for this exact scenario" (Vision-Map.md L310-319) — Eight-way distinction (also Availability, Performance, Maturity); "A competent person may lack authority" — conflation is the named failure mode (L321).

## SEMANTIC-INVARIANTS

- "artifact produced ≠ professional obligation satisfied ≠ parent outcome recomposed ≠ authorized acceptance ≠ real-world outcome achieved ≠ capability demonstrated for future scenarios" (Vision-Map.md L91-98) — The six-link non-equivalence chain; the vision's core firewall against artifact-as-success ("Artifacts ... are not the Outcome itself", L87).
- "Professional Scenario ≠ external reality / ≠ PWA / ≠ Undertaking / ≠ Professional Work Graph / ≠ plan or workflow / ≠ case-file repository / ≠ complete digital twin / ≠ Narrative Memory / ≠ LLM context window" (Vision-Map.md L242-252) — Nine non-equivalences bounding the Scenario concept; "≠ LLM context window" blocks the obvious mis-implementation.
- "provider declaration ≠ capability exists ≠ capability is current ≠ capability applies to this scenario ≠ capability has sufficient capacity ≠ capability is authorized ≠ capability will succeed" (Vision-Map.md L342-350) — A capability declaration is a Claim requiring Evidence and qualified Assessment (L352); likewise composition: qualified constituents "≠ automatically qualified parent capability" (L358-365) and Agent Card ≠ capability proof, guardrail ≠ Assurance Engineering (L922-928).
- "Authentication, technical authorization, professional authority, ownership, and assurance remain distinct. Access to privileged material does not authorize an agent to waive privilege, approve a filing, prescribe treatment, accept a construction inspection, or bind an organization." (Vision-Map.md L1087) — Access ≠ authority; five control concepts that must never merge; agent delegations carry explicit prohibitions on professional Decisions and external Actions (L1085).

## PROTOCOL-PRACTICE

- "Scenario- or matter-private memory ... organization-private capability memory ... consortium or federation memory ... shareable professional knowledge ... civilizational knowledge. Movement among these classes is a protected transformation, not automatic training." (Vision-Map.md L599-605) — Five Narrative Memory isolation classes; class-crossing is governed, never implicit; protected experience never silently becomes shared capability (L1172).
- "Undertaking experience and observed Outcomes → scenario-specific reflection → candidate capability lesson → privilege, confidentiality, consent, and leakage review → representativeness and causal-limitations review → independent assurance → authorized ... revision → new version and requalification" (Vision-Map.md L609-618) — The governed learning path; "No later abstraction erases the source history. No generalized lesson becomes professional truth merely because it was learned frequently." (L622).

## OPEN-QUESTIONS-CONTRADICTIONS

- The document declares itself non-normative and "not an implementation constitution" (L5, L14), yet issues binding-sounding rules: scenario transformations "remain subject to the universal de minimis assurance floor, including Reasoning Review" (L276) and Janumi "must never assume" full knowledge capture (L718). Which "must" clauses restate existing canon versus assert new vision-only norms is unresolved; synthesis must adjudicate each.
- Horizon 7's stated purpose is ensuring "near-term architecture does not foreclose long-term stewardship" (L1154), yet L18 forbids candidate concepts from influencing implementation before deliberate semantic design and governance. Tension: architecture cannot both ignore the concepts and be shaped to avoid foreclosing them; no resolution mechanism is given.
