# Cycle 000 Ratification Instrument (C000-S-01..16)

**Format ruling (REG-D-013):** this is a full-judgment instrument, not a consent form. Every item carries: the proposed change (concrete text where short), the motivating finding with its verification status, the **strongest opposing consideration** (refuters' arguments where they exist; the drafter's steelman where they don't), and the consequence of each disposition. Recommendations accompany the material; they never substitute for it. Dispositions: **RATIFY / AMEND (state the text) / REJECT / DEFER** (current text stands; the item remains an open register question). There is no bulk disposition.

**Evidence-quality note:** the three HIGH findings this cycle were all refuted under adversarial verification; every item below derives from MEDIUM/LOW findings that received **one judge and no refutation pass** (the Cycle-001 machinery adds MEDIUM vetting). Where refuter material exists on the same seam (S-01), it is attached. Treat single-judge evidence as exactly that.
