## 5. Shakedown assessment of the harness itself (before Cycle 001)

The shakedown's headline result is about the machinery, not the canon: the canon held up better than the judges' first readings — every HIGH died under adversarial refutation. Changes recommended before Cycle 001:

1. **The refutation round is structurally one-sided — add a rebuttal/adjudication step.** 0/3 HIGH survival with two refuters each and no reply from the finding side means refutation is currently decisive by construction. Either give the original judge one bounded rebuttal, or add a neutral adjudicator who reads finding + refutations and rules. Without this, we cannot distinguish "the canon is sound" from "refuters out-write judges," and a future *true* HIGH may die the same way.
2. **Calibrate the HIGH bar with a pre-refutation self-test.** All three HIGHs shared a failure shape: a "text as written permits X" claim defeated by a closer good-faith reading (a dropped quantifier, an unread self-executing clause, a misread two-conjunct routing). Require judges, before assigning HIGH, to (a) quote the strongest opposing passage and (b) state why a good-faith reader still escapes it. This is the judge-side analogue of the canon's own non-example discipline.
3. **Add cross-probe collation to Plan.** The §2.1/§9.1 seam surfaced independently in three probes at three severities; harness §1 says repeated wobble on one passage is itself a canon finding, but no phase aggregates by passage. Plan should key surviving findings by cited passage and promote recurrences explicitly (as done manually in note a).
4. **Pre-flight oracle citation checks in Shape.** F23 (CONS-2's oracle citing off-point authority) would have caused a false docking by a literal judge. Shape should verify every oracle citation resolves and is on-point before agents run — the same discipline the canon demands of its own cross-references.
5. **Lightweight refutation for amendment-bearing MEDIUMs.** Eleven unverified MEDIUMs now feed a 16-item sponsor batch. At least those MEDIUMs whose disposition is a canon amendment (S-01..S-10) deserve a single cheap refuter pass, or the sponsor batch carries unvetted proposals — F4 shows the risk: its premise was already partially undercut by F2's refuters.
6. **Split the verdict vocabulary.** PARTIAL currently conflates "agent deviated from oracle" (CONS-2) with "agent exceeded the text but the text under-forces" (ECON-2, judged 'PASS behavior' yet PARTIAL) with "substance clean, bookkeeping stale" (CONSIST-1). Record two axes per probe — agent-conformance (pass/partial/fail vs oracle) and canon-fitness (clean/friction/defect) — so the regression baseline measures the corpus, not the probe agent's diligence.
7. **Probe-agent template hardening** (C000-A-05): absence claims require content greps. Two probes produced falsifiable negative search claims; both were caught downstream only by judge diligence and redundant canon design.
8. **Keep the refuters.** Genuinely: the adversarial-refutation design is the only reason this report queues 16 measured proposals instead of 3 false constitutional emergencies. It is the harness's proven component; items 1–2 tune it, they do not soften it.
